Wren iOS App Icon Pack

Recommended:
- Use AppIcon.appiconset in your Xcode Assets.xcassets folder, or
- Drag Wren-AppIcon-1024.png into the App Icon asset in Xcode.

- 1024x1024 RGB PNG master (no alpha)
- Modern AppIcon.appiconset
- Common legacy iPhone/iPad icon pixel sizes
